Leyline shows your repositories, branches, worktrees and the agents working in them as one live graph, in 2D or 3D.

It reads workspace and agent state from the Paseo daemon and draws it in a hub screen (all projects) and a workspace panel (one worktree). Clicking a node opens the matching agent or workspace.

Requires Paseo 0.15.2 or later on desktop or web. The graph renders with WebGL and is not available on iOS or Android.
